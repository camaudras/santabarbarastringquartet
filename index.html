A Santa Barbara original

Beautiful music. Calm preparation. A ceremony that feels like you.

The Santa Barbara String Quartet has provided live music for thousands of couples since 1985. Our repertoire spans traditional wedding music, classical favorites and contemporary covers, and we are happy to fulfill special requests so your music feels personal to your celebration.

Choose the full quartet or a more intimate trio, duo or soloist. We can shape the ensemble around your venue, guest count, musical vision and the atmosphere you want to create.
