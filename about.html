About the ensemble

World-class musicianship, with a Santa Barbara soul.

The Santa Barbara String Quartet has been providing live music for thousands of couples over the past 30 years.

Violin · Manager

Emily Sommermann

Violinist and SBSQ manager Emily Sommermann brings decades of orchestral, chamber and teaching experience to the ensemble. Trained in New York, she earned her Bachelor of Music from SUNY Albany and Master of Music from SUNY Stony Brook.

Her performance background includes the Albany, Long Island and Springfield symphonies as well as extensive work in Santa Barbara, including the Santa Barbara Symphony, Santa Barbara Chamber Orchestra and Santa Barbara Chamber Players.

Violin

Anna Corcoran

Santa Barbara native Anna Corcoran is a professional violinist and teacher whose work spans classical performance, chamber music and fiddle.

She earned her Bachelor of Music from the San Francisco Conservatory of Music and her Master of Music from UCLA.

Viola

Cam Audras

Cam Audras is a Santa Barbara-based violist and educator whose performance work ranges from orchestral and chamber music to collaborations with artists including Pink Martini and Judy Collins.

He performs across California with organizations including the Santa Barbara Symphony, Opera Santa Barbara, Santa Cruz Symphony and Santa Barbara Chamber Players.

Cello

Jacqueline Greenshields

Jacqueline “Jackie” Greenshields has been part of Santa Barbara's professional music community since the 1980s.

She is a longtime member of the Santa Barbara Symphony and previously served for decades as principal cellist of the Santa Barbara Chamber Orchestra.

Exceptional players. A celebration that feels effortless.
