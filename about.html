About the ensemble

World-class musicianship, with a Santa Barbara soul.

The Santa Barbara String Quartet has been providing live music for thousands of couples over the past 30 years.

Our extensive repertoire ranges from traditional wedding music to contemporary covers. We can perform as a mini-orchestra, quartet, trio, duo or solo instrumentalists, and we are happy to fulfill special requests to create a tailored entertainment experience.

Our musicians

Concert-hall artistry. Wedding-day ease.

Our musicians bring decades of orchestral, chamber, teaching and recording experience to every event — with the flexibility and attention to detail a wedding day requires.

Violin · Manager

Emily Sommermann

Violinist and SBSQ manager Emily Sommermann brings decades of orchestral, chamber and teaching experience to the ensemble. Trained in New York, she earned her Bachelor of Music from SUNY Albany and Master of Music from SUNY Stony Brook, studying with musicians including Isidor Cohen of the Beaux Arts Trio.

Her performance background includes the Albany, Long Island and Springfield symphonies as well as extensive work in Santa Barbara, including the Santa Barbara Symphony, Santa Barbara Chamber Orchestra, Santa Barbara Civic Light Opera Orchestra and Santa Barbara Chamber Players. Emily is also an Instructor of Violin at Westmont College and directs chamber music at Cate School. She leads SBSQ with a focus on polished playing, thoughtful preparation and a relaxed experience for clients.

Violin

Anna Corcoran

Santa Barbara native Anna Corcoran is a professional violinist and teacher whose work spans classical performance, chamber music and fiddle. She earned her Bachelor of Music from the San Francisco Conservatory of Music and her Master of Music from UCLA, where she served as first violinist of the VEM String Quartet and performed internationally.

Anna performs throughout Southern California with ensembles including New West Symphony, Orchestra Santa Monica, Long Beach Symphony and Santa Barbara String Quartet. Alongside performing, she maintains an active teaching practice and works across classical, Irish and Scottish fiddle traditions. Her stylistic versatility makes her equally at home in traditional ceremony repertoire and contemporary wedding arrangements.

Viola

Cam Audras

Cam Audras is a Santa Barbara-based violist and educator whose performance work ranges from orchestral and chamber music to collaborations with artists including Pink Martini and Judy Collins. He performs across California with organizations including the Santa Barbara Symphony, Opera Santa Barbara, Santa Cruz Symphony and Santa Barbara Chamber Players.

Cam holds degrees in viola performance and environmental studies from USC and master's degrees in viola performance and environmental science and management from UC Santa Barbara. He was named the USC Thornton School of Music's outstanding undergraduate in strings and has appeared at festivals including Bowdoin, Manchester Music Festival, Credo at Oberlin and Center Stage Strings. Cam serves on the viola faculty at Westmont College and maintains a private teaching studio.

Cello

Jacqueline Greenshields

Jacqueline “Jackie” Greenshields has been part of Santa Barbara's professional music community since the 1980s. She is a longtime member of the Santa Barbara Symphony and previously served for decades as principal cellist of the Santa Barbara Chamber Orchestra. Her career has also included orchestral work in Australia, European touring with The American Sinfonietta and summers at the Music Academy of the West.

Jackie earned degrees from the University of Oklahoma and USC and remains active as a chamber musician and teacher. She brings an exceptional depth of ensemble experience to SBSQ, pairing the warmth and foundation of the cello with the attentiveness and flexibility that live event music demands.
