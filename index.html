A Santa Barbara original

Beautiful music. Calm preparation. A ceremony that feels like you.

The Santa Barbara String Quartet has provided live music for thousands of couples over the past 30 years. Our repertoire spans traditional wedding music, classical favorites and contemporary covers, and we are happy to fulfill special requests so your music feels personal to your celebration.

Choose the full quartet or a more intimate trio, duo or soloist. We can shape the ensemble around your venue, guest count, musical vision and the atmosphere you want to create.

From aisle to cocktail hour

Music that moves with your day.

We can perform throughout your celebration and coordinate with your planner or venue team so musical transitions feel natural and polished.

  1. Guest arrival Set the atmosphere with welcoming prelude music before the ceremony begins.
  2. Ceremony Processional, bridal entrance, interludes and recessional — timed to the movement of the day.
  3. Cocktail hour & beyond Classical favorites, pop covers, jazz, rock and other selections can keep the energy flowing after “I do.”
